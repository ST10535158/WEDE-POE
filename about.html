Who we are

Cape Bloom Bakery is a fictional small bakery based in Cape Town, South Africa. The bakery specialises in freshly prepared baked goods for individuals, families and local events.

The business aims to provide a convenient customer experience by combining quality products with clear information and simple online enquiries.
